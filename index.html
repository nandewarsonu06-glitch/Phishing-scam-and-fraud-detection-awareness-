01 • KNOW THE THREATS

What is phishing & fraud?

Attackers often use trust, urgency and impersonation to make people take unsafe actions.

🎣

Phishing

Fake messages or websites that imitate trusted entities to steal information.

💬

Smishing

Phishing delivered through SMS or messaging platforms.

📞

Vishing

Fraudulent calls using impersonation, pressure or fear.

🧠

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing information or taking an unsafe action.

💳

Financial Fraud

Deceptive or unauthorized activity intended to cause monetary loss.

🔑

Account Theft

Credentials or personal details are obtained to access or misuse an account.

03 • PROTECT YOURSELF

Your safety checklist

STOPDon't click, pay, download or reply immediately.
CHECKInspect the sender, number, domain and context.
VERIFYUse the official app, website or trusted contact independently.
PROTECTUse strong unique passwords and multi-factor authentication.
REPORTPreserve evidence and use official reporting channels when needed.
Never share

OTP • UPI PIN • CVV • Password • Recovery code • Remote-access code

Always verify

Recipient name • Amount • Website domain • Sender identity • Unexpected requests

IF FRAUD HAPPENS

Act quickly. Don't panic.

Stop further communication or payments, preserve screenshots and transaction details, contact the relevant financial service through official channels, secure compromised accounts, and use appropriate official cyber-fraud reporting channels.
